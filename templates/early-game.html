Use every recruit turn

Fast decision loop

  1. Count the next combat: board slots, raw stats, shields, summons and whether the opponent looks greedy.
  2. Check the shop before refreshing: a pair of cards that work now is usually worth more than one speculative tribal name.
  3. Spend for the weakest point: fill empty slots, replace dead bodies, then improve concentrated carries.
  4. Level only when the board can absorb it: health is a resource, but repeated double-digit hits are not a curve.
  5. Ask whether you have Core: one early synergy is tempo. A repeatable payoff with supply and a credible transition is direction.

Season 14 economy

Dark Gifts change the curve

From Turn 3, the Dark Gift button costs 3 Gold and Discovers one of three minions paired with a Gift. It can be used once per turn, up to three times per game, and later offers improve.

Take it now whenthe offered minion/Gift materially fixes this combat or your next turn without wrecking the board.
Wait whenyour board is stable and spending 3 Gold would replace a strong buy, delay a necessary level, or leave a hole.
Do not autopilot“later is better” is value logic, not permission to lose half your health before the offer improves.

Activate: these minions spend the printed Gold during Recruit Phase. Each minion’s Activate can be used once per turn. Buying an Activate body does not mean you must pay for it immediately.

Conditional, not ceremonial

Curve selector

Level

Choose it when: your current board is competitive, the shop is weak, and the higher tier contains enough useful improvements.

Warning: avoid levelling into multiple empty or clearly obsolete slots unless health and opponent let you absorb the hit.

Buy

Choose it when: the shop offers two efficient bodies, a real mini-synergy, or a stabilizer that improves several existing minions.

Good delay: staying down for one strong turn is fine when it prevents repeated damage and leaves a clean level next turn.

Roll

Choose it rarely: only after checking every useful purchase, with Gold left to buy the result and a narrow immediate need.

Bad roll: searching Tier 1–3 for a named future-comp card while the shop already contains playable tempo.

Dark Gift

Choose it when: the offer’s immediate body plus Gift beats the best normal use of those 3 Gold.

Wait when: the board is safe and you are preserving a use for stronger later offerings.

When you are taking heavy damage

Emergency stabilization

  1. Stop following the planned curve. Spend this turn on combat power unless the level itself unlocks a clearly stronger shop and still leaves buying Gold.
  2. Fill the board. Summons, Divine Shields and Reborn bodies frequently prevent more damage than one speculative scaler.
  3. Concentrate permanent buffs. Make one or two minions win trades rather than decorating seven disposable bodies.
  4. Use paid abilities deliberately. Prisonguard, Fruit Vendor and similar Activate cards are valuable when their effect changes combat—not because the button is glowing.
  5. Sell the fantasy. A held pair or future addon that costs a live board slot is not an investment when elimination is close.

Avoid these

Early-game traps

  • Refreshing for tribe density instead of buying present strength.
  • Calling one tribal payoff a commitment before it has fuel.
  • Buying economy whose weak body makes the next combat catastrophic.
  • Keeping several pairs while fielding fewer effective combat bodies.
  • Paying every Activate automatically.
  • Taking a Dark Gift simply because the button became available.

Before opening a comp guide

Pivot to Core checklist

  • Do I have the actual payoff, not merely a shared tribe?
  • Can my board or hand supply its resource repeatedly?
  • Can I transition without selling most of my current combat strength at once?
  • Is the key Tavern tier reachable before another large hit?
  • If the next shop misses, can this board still survive?

Mostly “no”? Keep the tempo package and stay open. Your early board did its job; it does not need a retirement plan.
