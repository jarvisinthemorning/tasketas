## Game plan

Build a large stat reservoir with [[card:133081|Barrier Banshee]], move those stats through [[card:120104|Drustfallen Butcher]], and use [[card:133083|Snazzy Phantom]] to convert Reborn attack into additional growth on the rightmost Undead.

[[card:113158|Cadaver Caretaker]] is an early Deathrattle bridge visible in the source, but the mature board revolves around the Banshee–Butcher–Snazzy chain.

## How to build it

- **Bridge:** take outside-combat destruction and Reborn pieces that preserve tempo.
- **Commit:** Banshee plus a way to transfer or reuse its stats is the signal.
- **Scale:** set up Reborn before relying on Snazzy Phantom; the source repeatedly checks that requirement.
- **Finish:** keep the intended recipient as the rightmost Undead and remove weak units that interfere with the chain.

## Positioning and traps

Rightmost placement is functional, not cosmetic. A last-second utility swap can redirect an entire turn of scaling to the wrong body.

Do not treat Snazzy as a standalone carry. Without Reborn attack to convert, it is only occupying a premium slot and looking pleased with itself.

## Jailer–Plaguerunner variant

[dogdog's Jailer game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZUuFKCLgS4o&t=830s) shows a stronger hero-specific route into the same Snazzy conversion engine. [[card:133391|Plaguerunner Portrait]] makes outside-combat Plaguerunner sacrifices replace themselves, while [[card:131133|Jailer Sticker]] turns disposable Undead into two fresh bodies. That extra generation makes it easier to find Reborn fuel and keep [[card:120104|Drustfallen Butcher]] active without freezing the board.

The important sequencing clarification comes at [17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZUuFKCLgS4o&t=1070s): Butchering can build the attack bank, but Snazzy copies the Attack of the **Reborn unit**, not the stats the unit had before dying. Position the intended recipient as your rightmost Undead, preserve enough actual Reborn bodies, and do not count a giant pre-death body as guaranteed Snazzy value.

## Demonstrated payoff

The source wins the lobby and the final recruit phase at 21:47 visibly shows permanent bodies at 924/773, 604/469, and 335/245 before combat. Undead does not need to imitate a pure shop-scaling tribe: the guide is valid because the Reborn transfer chain is online, the recipient order is deliberate, and the board converts those stats into a first-place combat—not because every slot crosses an arbitrary four-digit threshold.

The additional dogdog source reinforces the same finish: Snazzy appears around **13:56**, the Reborn-stat interaction is explained at **17:50**, and the run closes first at **25:44**.
