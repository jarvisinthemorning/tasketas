## Game plan

Build a dense board of [[card:127446|Timewarped Embalmer]] copies with Reborn, then let them repeatedly refresh one another's Reborn-granting effect during combat. [[card:90425|Leeroy the Reckless]] converts those extra summons into removal: it trades into a target, returns, and can do it again.

This is a real composition, not merely two emergency scam cards. XQN commits to the engine, reaches the final opponent, plays around disruption with redundant payoff units, and wins the lobby.

## When to commit

Commit when you already have multiple Embalmers, can put Reborn on the engine, and have enough health to replace ordinary tempo with redundancy. One isolated Embalmer is not a composition; the source only treats the board as online after the Reborn chain is established.

Do not rely on a single Leeroy. XQN explicitly prefers two removal bodies or a mixed package such as Leeroy plus [[card:65031|Deadly Spore]]. That redundancy is what turns the line from a clever screenshot into a board that survives awkward attacks.

## How to build it

- **Assemble the engine:** collect Embalmers and prioritize giving Reborn to every copy that will stay on the final board.
- **Bridge:** use [[card:126966|Heroic Underdog]] for temporary tempo, but sell it once a second real removal body is available.
- **Add the first kill:** use a taunted Reborn Leeroy when possible so combat starts by trading a removal piece rather than exposing the support line.
- **Add redundancy:** a second Leeroy is best. Deadly Spore is the clean Tavern Tier 3 substitute when the second Leeroy does not appear.
- **Use Murloc utility:** [[card:122219|Bile Spitter]] is a bulkier Venomous option and can grant Venomous to another Murloc. XQN uses it as a Spore-like replacement rather than pretending it is a scaling carry.
- **Stop buying stats:** once the chain is online, additional scam bodies and correct keywords matter more than making the Embalmers impressively medium-sized.

## Positioning

Keep the first reliable removal body early and preserve a second payoff behind it. The source's practical rule is redundancy: two Leeroys, or Leeroy plus Spore/Bile, so one disrupted attack does not end the entire plan.

Taunt is useful on the intended first Leeroy, but avoid random taunts that let the opponent control which support unit dies first. Recheck the opponent every round; this board wins through combat order, not decorative green numbers.

## Failure modes

- **Only one payoff:** if the first Leeroy is neutralized, the remaining Embalmers may repeatedly summon bodies that do not remove anything meaningful.
- **Incomplete Reborn coverage:** an Embalmer returning without the intended chain can consume board space without refreshing the next summon.
- **Too many enemy summons:** repeated shields, Reborn units, and token waves can exhaust the removal chain before the real carries are gone.
- **Forcing from one piece:** buying every Embalmer-shaped object while dying is still dying, only with a theme.

## Duos use

Every listed card is current in both Solo and Duos. In Duos, this package is most useful as the utility warband beside a partner's conventional stat board: the scam side removes oversized enemy carries while the partner preserves the team's stable midgame and cleans up summon-heavy boards.
