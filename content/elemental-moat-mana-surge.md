## Game plan

Trigger [[card:132981|Moat Custodian]] early and often so every later Elemental buff becomes larger. Once [[card:120674|Unleashed Mana Surge]] arrives, cycle Elementals to convert that accumulated amplification into repeated board-wide growth.

The reviewed game uses recruit-phase Rally on a Golden Custodian before leaning into multiple Mana Surges. [[card:133455|Air Baller]] and [[card:116736|Fire Baller]] are useful bodies. [[card:64077|Tavern Tempest]], [[card:119949|Sand Swirler]], and [[card:119951|Glowing Cinder]] are concrete source-backed cycle fuel, but Custodian is what raises the ceiling.

## How to build it

- **Bridge:** buy Elementals that preserve economy or tempo; do not cycle weak units for negligible buffs.
- **Commit:** Custodian amplification plus a repeatable buff outlet is the signal.
- **Scale:** trigger Custodian before spending the turn's Elemental resources whenever possible.
- **Finish:** keep enough open space to continue cycling while protecting the largest permanent bodies.

## Practical cautions

Sequence matters. Cycling first and amplifying later wastes the strongest part of the engine. The source explicitly compares low-value Sand Swirler cycling with the much larger return from additional Custodian triggers.

This is not a Nomi shop-scaling guide: the stats come from increasing buff magnitude, then repeatedly applying those buffs.
