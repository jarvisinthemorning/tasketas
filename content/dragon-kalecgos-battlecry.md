## Game plan

Pair [[card:60630|Kalecgos, Arcane Aspect]] with [[card:96786|Brann Bronzebeard]], then spend the recruit turn cycling Battlecries rather than freezing a static board. Each useful Battlecry becomes both immediate value and permanent Dragon scaling.

The source uses [[card:132955|Bronze Timewalker]] to accelerate the line, [[card:133329|Gatekeeper Amalgam]] as a flexible typed body, and [[card:101314|Drakkari Enchanter]] when the board has meaningful end-of-turn value.

## How to build it

- **Bridge:** preserve economy and Battlecry generators; do not force Dragons before finding a payoff.
- **Commit:** Kalecgos plus Brann is the clean signal. Either card alone is usually only a direction hint.
- **Scale:** cycle the cheapest useful Battlecries first and keep one flexible shop slot.
- **Finish:** replace low-stat economy pieces only when the remaining turns no longer justify their value.

## Positioning and traps

Keep Brann away from obvious opening cleave exposure. Gatekeeper can carry stats and defensive keywords, but it does not replace the Battlecry engine.

The common failure is buying every Dragon and ending the turn with unused gold. This build grows by actions, not by admiring its own board.
