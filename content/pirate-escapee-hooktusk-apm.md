## Game plan

[[card:132762|Enterprising Escapee]] advances Lockboxes as gold is spent. Their Golden minions and discovers feed [[card:132925|Hooktusk, Master Marauder]], while [[card:122516|Sky Admiral Rogers]] and [[card:122520|Proud Privateer]] keep the economy moving. [[card:132921|Clever Castaway]] helps bridge into the cycle.

The source gives the recipe directly, then shows the engine becoming effectively self-sustaining once Hooktusk and Privateer are online.

## How to build it

- **Bridge:** take Pirate economy without filling all seven slots.
- **Commit:** Escapee plus reliable spending and Lockbox progression is the signal.
- **Scale:** convert Golden rewards through Hooktusk and keep chaining resources quickly.
- **Finish:** cut obsolete economy—Rogers included—when cleave, protection, or scam improves the last fights more.

## Execution and traps

Always preserve one cycling slot. Plan buys, sells, and discoveries before animations consume the recruit timer.

The source explicitly warns that the comp is mechanically demanding. If the turn begins with no plan, the client will happily spend fifteen seconds showing you sparkles while your gold expires. Very festive; not especially useful.
