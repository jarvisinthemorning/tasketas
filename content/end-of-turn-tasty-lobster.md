## Description

A [[card:132796|Tasty Lobster]] engine that turns repeated Deathrattle triggers into permanent Beast scaling. The normal shell uses [[card:132800|Headhunter Gryphon]] and [[card:132808|Deathstrider]] to retrigger the left-most Deathrattle during combat, while [[card:97408|Titus Rivendare]] doubles both the immediate buffs and the growth of future Lobsters.

The reviewed game adds an unusually powerful high-roll: [[card:133400|Wolfhead Flail]] plus a copied Flail from [[card:112895|Souvenir Stand]] trigger every friendly Deathrattle twice at the end of the turn. [[card:101314|Drakkari Enchanter]] repeats those end-of-turn effects, and Titus repeats the Deathrattles themselves.

## When to commit

Do not commit just because you found one Lobster. The video explicitly shows another board failing after forcing “Lobster comp” without a way to repeatedly trigger or generate Lobsters.

The line becomes real when an early [[card:132796|Tasty Lobster]] is paired with at least one practical engine:

- [[card:132800|Headhunter Gryphon]] to generate Beasts and provide Rally attacks.
- [[card:133039|Hoarding Hyena]] to summon additional Lobsters during the Tavern phase.
- [[card:97408|Titus Rivendare]] to multiply every Lobster trigger.
- [[card:132808|Deathstrider]] once your Rally attackers and left-most Deathrattle are established.
- [[card:133400|Wolfhead Flail]] as the exceptional end-of-turn route shown in this game.

## Enablers

- An early Lobster provides useful tempo while its future copies scale.
- Reborn on Lobster gives another combat trigger.
- Windfury on a Rally attacker creates more Rally attacks and therefore more Deathstrider triggers.
- Gryphon-generated Beasts supply additional pieces and disposable resources.
- Hoarding Hyena can print Lobsters, but it needs open board space when its Rally triggers.

## How to play

1. **Scale future Lobsters early.** Reborn the first Lobsters when offered; their Deathrattles improve subsequent copies as well as the current board.
2. **Build repeatable triggers rather than a pile of Beasts.** Gryphon and Hyena generate the material, while Titus and Deathstrider turn it into a scaling engine.
3. **Use Rally attacks as activators.** With Deathstrider on board, each friendly Rally minion attack triggers your left-most Deathrattle.
4. **Keep enough board space.** Hyena cannot produce another Lobster if the board is full.
5. **Treat Drakkari as conditional.** It is excellent with the double end-of-turn Flail setup from this game, but it is not automatically part of every Lobster board.

## Positioning

Place the best [[card:132796|Tasty Lobster]] on the far left when using Deathstrider. In the reviewed game, placing the non-golden Lobster left-most lost value because Deathstrider repeatedly selected it instead of the golden copy.

Let Rally minions such as Gryphon attack early enough to activate Deathstrider. Keep Titus and, when relevant, Drakkari protected behind the attacking engine.

## Cycle and temporary pieces

[[card:132802|Fishbait]] and random Beasts generated by Gryphon are resources rather than the final objective. Use them to maintain tempo, create favorable Deathrattles and find additional Lobsters without sacrificing the engine slots.

## Failure modes

- Forcing Lobster without Titus, Deathstrider, Gryphon, Hyena or another repeatable trigger.
- Filling the board before a Hyena Rally can summon a Lobster.
- Letting Deathstrider target a weaker left-most Deathrattle.
- Keeping Drakkari without meaningful end-of-turn effects.
- Spending too much gold perfecting the dream board when the existing permanent scaling is already enough to win the next combat.
