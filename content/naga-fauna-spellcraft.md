## Game plan

Use [[card:119942|Tranquil Meditative]] to build Spellcraft power, then let [[card:120905|Fauna Whisperer]] repeatedly cast its targeted scaling at end of turn. [[card:101314|Drakkari Enchanter]] multiplies those end-of-turn casts, turning one source of spell power into board-wide growth.

[[card:103069|Sea Witch Zar'jira]] and [[card:99054|Zesty Shaker]] support spell duplication. [[card:114816|Groundbreaker]] is the source's resilient anti-scam target because full-stat Reborn makes it harder for one Venomous trade to erase the turn's investment.

## How to build it

- **Bridge:** use early Spellcraft tempo and preserve Meditative's scaling.
- **Commit:** Fauna plus spell power and at least one extra end-of-turn trigger is the clean signal.
- **Scale:** maximize the number of relevant Nagas before Fauna resolves, then duplicate the strongest Spellcraft effects.
- **Finish:** consolidate protection and Reborn onto a carry without cutting the spell-power engine too early.

## Positioning and traps

Keep Groundbreaker where its Reborn body protects the rest of the board rather than being wasted into an irrelevant first hit.

Fauna without spell power is underwhelming; Drakkari without a meaningful end-of-turn effect is just a small neutral. Assemble the interaction, not a museum display of its parts.
